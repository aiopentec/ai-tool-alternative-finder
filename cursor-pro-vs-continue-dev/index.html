Overview

Cursor Pro and Continue are AI tools designed to enhance productivity and workflow. Both tools cater to individuals and teams seeking to streamline their tasks, but they differ in their approach and benefits. Users of Cursor Pro may benefit from switching to Continue for its cost-effective and privacy-focused solution.

Key Differences

  • Cost: Continue is free, while Cursor Pro requires a monthly subscription.
  • Privacy/Data: Continue offers local processing and self-hosting, whereas Cursor Pro sends data to vendor servers.
  • Setup Difficulty: Continue may require more technical expertise for setup due to its self-hosted nature, whereas Cursor Pro is more straightforward.
  • Quality Parity: Both tools offer high-quality AI-powered features, but the quality may vary depending on specific use cases.
  • Ecosystem: Continue has an open-source ecosystem with an Apache 2.0 license, while Cursor Pro has a proprietary license.

When to Choose Each

Cursor Pro is ideal for users who prioritize ease of use and are willing to pay a monthly subscription fee for a proprietary solution. It is suitable for individuals and teams who require high-quality AI-powered features and dedicated customer support, but do not have strict data privacy and security requirements.

Continue is ideal for users who value data privacy and security, and are looking for a cost-effective solution. It is suitable for individuals and teams who are technically inclined and can handle the setup and maintenance of a self-hosted solution, and who require a high degree of control over their data.
