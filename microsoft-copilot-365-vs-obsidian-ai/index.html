Overview

Microsoft Copilot 365 and Obsidian + Smart Connections are two distinct AI-powered tools catering to different user needs. Microsoft Copilot 365 is designed for businesses and individuals already invested in the Microsoft ecosystem, while Obsidian + Smart Connections offers a self-hosted, privacy-focused alternative. Users seeking more control over their data and costs may benefit from switching to Obsidian + Smart Connections.

Key Differences

  • Cost: Microsoft Copilot 365 requires a monthly subscription, whereas Obsidian + Smart Connections is free with optional self-hosting costs.
  • Privacy/Data: Microsoft Copilot 365 sends data to vendor servers, while Obsidian + Smart Connections processes data locally or on self-hosted servers.
  • Setup Difficulty: Microsoft Copilot 365 is generally easier to set up, given its integration with existing Microsoft services, while Obsidian + Smart Connections requires more technical expertise.
  • Quality Parity: Both tools offer high-quality AI-powered features, but Microsoft Copilot 365 has more extensive integration with Microsoft apps.
  • Ecosystem: Microsoft Copilot 365 is tightly integrated with the Microsoft ecosystem, while Obsidian + Smart Connections is part of a broader, more customizable note-taking and knowledge management ecosystem.

Pros and Cons

Microsoft Copilot 365:
  • Easy integration with Microsoft apps and services
  • Robust AI-powered features and support
  • Regular updates and improvements
  • High cost, especially for large teams or organizations
Obsidian + Smart Connections:
  • Free, with optional self-hosting costs
  • Local data processing and self-hosting options for enhanced privacy
  • Customizable and extensible through plugins and community support
  • Steeper learning curve due to technical requirements

When to Choose Each

Microsoft Copilot 365 is ideal for businesses and individuals already deeply invested in the Microsoft ecosystem, requiring seamless integration with Microsoft apps and services. Obsidian + Smart Connections, on the other hand, is suited for users who prioritize data privacy, customization, and cost savings, and are willing to invest time in setting up and configuring their note-taking and knowledge management system.

Migration / Getting Started

To switch from Microsoft Copilot 365 to Obsidian + Smart Connections, follow these steps:
  1. Export your data from Microsoft Copilot 365, if possible, and prepare it for import into Obsidian.
  2. Set up Obsidian on your local machine or self-hosted server, and install the Smart Connections plugin.
  3. Configure Obsidian and Smart Connections to meet your specific needs, exploring plugins and community resources as needed.
